Who it is for

Two reasons to renovate, one standard of execution.

For your own use

You want a property that meets your expectations in design and comfort, without coordinating contractors from a distance. I plan the works, direct the site and deliver the finished property.

Ahead of a sale

Buyers of mountain property tend to favour homes that are ready to occupy. A dated interior lengthens the time on the market and weighs on the price. A targeted renovation positions the property to sell sooner and on better terms.

Case study

Ovronnaz: a dated apartment, sold at the full asking price.

A 54.8 m² apartment acquired for CHF 310,000. Works of CHF 64,000 completed in eight weeks: partition walls removed, kitchen and bathroom replaced, oak flooring and reclaimed-wood wall finishes installed. The property sold at its full asking price of CHF 460,000 within two months.

Process

Four stages, one point of contact.

01

Initial consultation

We begin with a conversation about your property and your objectives, followed by a site visit. This allows me to understand your project in full and to gather the information required to prepare a plan of action.

02

Proposal

On a second call, I present a draft proposal setting out the plan of action, schedule, cost range and fees. Your feedback is incorporated into the final written proposal, which is agreed before work begins.

03

Execution

I handle the permits, coordinate the contractors and supervise the site. You receive regular written progress reports with photographs.

04

Handover

You take possession of a completed property. If you are selling, your estate agent carries out the valuation and manages the sale. I can recommend an agent on request.

Frequently asked questions

Practical information.

Do I need to be on site?

No. I represent you on site throughout the works and report to you in writing at regular intervals.

Do you value or sell properties?

No. Valuation and sale remain the responsibility of your estate agent. I manage the renovation and coordinate with the agent where a sale is planned.

Is the service only for owners who intend to sell?

No. The service is the same whether you are renovating for your own use or ahead of a sale. It also applies if you are acquiring a property that requires renovation.

Where do you operate?

In Lower Valais and Central Valais. I am open to discussing projects in other locations.
